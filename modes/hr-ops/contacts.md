# Identify contacts

Read the dossier. Search official team pages and public company announcements for current names and roles. Startup priority: technical founders at very small firms; otherwise CTO, VP/Head of Engineering, Head of Talent or Recruiting Lead. Agency priority: founder/managing partner, Head of Delivery or Head of Recruiting.

Store a person's name, title, company-page URL, verification time and source IDs through research input. Never guess email formats; an email must occur in cited public evidence. Do not scrape LinkedIn, enrich through paid tools, or send connection requests. If only a role can be identified, record it as an unresolved research task rather than inventing a person.

Run `contacts COMPANY` for ranked results. Explain why the best person is relevant and disclose stale or unverified information. Finding contacts is authorized internal research; contacting them is not.
