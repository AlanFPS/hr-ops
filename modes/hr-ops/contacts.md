# Identify contacts

Read the dossier. Search official team pages, public company announcements, LinkedIn (search results, company People pages and profiles) and paid contact-data tools for current names and roles.

Look first for people below the C-level who are closer to day-to-day hiring and more likely to reply. Startups: engineering managers, tech or team leads, heads, directors or VPs of engineering, technical recruiters and talent acquisition or people leads. Agencies: recruiters, account or delivery managers and heads of recruiting or delivery. Use CEOs, CTOs, co-founders, managing partners and other executives only when no current lower-tier contact turns up, or when the company is so small that the founders are the hiring contacts; record them as a fallback even then. `contacts COMPANY` lists the reachable tier before executives.

Store a person's name, title, source URL, verification time and source IDs through research input. Never guess email addresses or email formats; an email must occur in cited evidence. Save the person's LinkedIn profile URL as evidence whose excerpt names them; the sender needs it to verify the recipient. A LinkedIn profile you actually opened is the person's own statement of their current title and employer and may be saved as `primary`. Search-result snippets and paid-tool records are `secondary`: they establish currency only with a date inside the contact freshness window, or once a profile or company page confirms them. Connection requests are sent only through `modes/hr-ops/linkedin-send.md` on the user's instruction, never as part of contact research. If only a role can be identified, record it as an unresolved research task rather than inventing a person.

Run `contacts COMPANY` for ranked results. Explain why the best person is relevant and disclose stale or unverified information. Finding contacts is authorized internal research; contacting them is not.

Use the remaining company page allowance, normally up to two pages after provisional admission. Prioritize a linked official team page or current leadership biography rather than another generic services page. A current relevant contact allows exploratory draft preparation; it does not establish supplier authority or willingness to partner. If the name is ambiguous, retain the uncertainty and do not label a draft ready for human use.

People/HR leadership, COOs and Managing Directors can be relevant depending on the ICP; verify actual responsibility rather than relying solely on title rank. For dated sources retain `published_at`. Secondary sources without a publication date cannot establish currency; dated primary or secondary sources must also fall within the contact freshness window. Prefer a current official team page over an old press article. Undated primary pages remain usable, but disclose that their publication/update age is unknown.

Agency commercial, partnerships and business-development leaders are secondary contacts below owners and recruiting/delivery leaders. Verify their supplier or delivery-partner remit; a commercial title alone does not establish buying authority. These titles do not establish engineering hiring ownership at a startup.
