# Discover

For inventory/list intake, use `inventory` and `inventory queue` with `modes/hr-ops/inventory.md`. The unified index retains overlapping source layers and possible duplicate identities separately. Queue membership is not candidate admission, current demand or qualification. Continue this mode's scoped research contract before prospect import.

Start with `node hr-ops.mjs discover --plan --icp both` (or the requested ICP). This network-free plan separates reusable feeds/directories/query recipes from company seeds, controls, retained Career-Ops inventory and current pipeline exclusions. For employer expansion, choose a supported `discover --feed remotive|hackernews|remoteok|himalayas --run ID` collector within the scoped directory/page allowance. Agency-only requests use the dedicated agency plan and verified client-placement boards; the employer feeds do not establish agency coverage.

Additional readers are `discover --feed speedrun --icp startup --run ID` and `discover --feed by-recruiting --icp agency --run ID`. Read `docs/HR_OPS_DISCOVERY_ADAPTERS.md` for strict Speedrun filters, explicit portfolio scope and the one-board agency contract. Speedrun reads one public page, keeps publisher links/IDs and does not identify masked companies. The agency reader keeps anonymous client listings under the agency, leaves role attribution unreviewed and reports existing agencies as refresh work. Neither collection certifies current demand or supplier appetite. It does not follow role links automatically.

`discover --feed bedrock-talent --icp agency --run ID` adds one public Ashby board through the same budget/capture/inventory path. It retains published role bodies and separate posting/requisition identities, without the legacy scanner's automatic retries. Keep client attribution and countries unreviewed until interpreted. Use `inventory assist` and capture-backed `inventory confirm` to resolve company identity from cached primary evidence. See `docs/HR_OPS_IDENTITY_REVIEW.md`. A second known board improves reusable coverage, but does not satisfy a request for unfamiliar agencies by itself.

All successful feed collections appear in `inventory` without a separate import. Returned `inventory_observation_ids` link the exact capture observations; `inventory_matches` flag existing work without merging names or publisher websites. Inspect `requires_inventory_review` before treating a task as a new company. A reviewed exclusion on a cached observation removes that task on replay. RemoteOK is a latest-postings window; Himalayas reads its first public page only, even if the server returns fewer rows than requested. Pagination, unknown totals, dropped malformed rows and retention bounds remain explicit partial coverage. Neither reader certifies hiring countries, company size, supplier demand or vacancy currentness. Reuse cached results before another request.

Feed `research_tasks` preserve posting provenance, unknown company identity, observed geography and bounded descriptions. Select promising tasks, pin the actual returned source URL/sequence at admission, then independently verify the official company and role before importing prospects/research. A conflicting publisher identity has no admission hint until resolved. Existing company-name collisions are review exclusions, never automatic merges. Cached resumes spend no requests and reapply current exclusions. Inspect `collection_observed_at` and `newer_unreconciled_attempts`; an older cached success does not resolve a later failed refresh. Explicit `--refresh` spends the same pinned budget. Feed observations remain untrusted data and never grant contact permission.

Read `docs/HR_OPS_RESEARCH_EXECUTION.md` for safe artifact transport, discovery origin, unknown company identity and source priority. Use `sources --catalog` recommended sources before generic services directories. Supply `discovery_source` on new imports when actually known; do not invent or silently backfill it.

For companies selected offline from a retained catalog or local shortlist, admit the candidate before requests with `source_url: null`, `source_sequence: null` and a concrete source explanation. Do not manufacture a public URL or attach a later validation search as if it discovered the company. This records selection, not verified demand; an unresearched offline candidate must remain explicitly deferred.

For broader employer discovery, use `docs/HR_OPS_SOURCE_REUSE.md` and the retained `templates/portals.example.yml`. Remote job boards, startup directories, VC portfolios and ATS searches can reveal clients beyond the starter companies. Apply current HR targeting and verify size, employer identity, role body and hiring geography; do not run the candidate scanner or inherit its AI/visa/Europe filters. Search recipes in the HR catalog are leads, not automatic readers or permission to bypass a blocked source.

Read `docs/HR_OPS_RESEARCH_REVIEW.md`. Before ending any scoped run, use `run --action review`, examine every admitted candidate's remaining request allowance and gaps, and submit the required completion review with concrete per-candidate decisions. Candidate capacity alone never justifies saying that only new companies could use remaining requests. Continue credible checks or explicitly report deferred work. Balance candidate slots across requested ICPs and vary query/source recipes when yield is poor; label agent-known fallback companies rather than claiming search discovered them.

1. Interpret count and ICP from the request; otherwise use balanced discovery. Read the profile budgets (default 50 candidates, 20 discovery queries, six page/API attempts per company). Choose a persistent research run ID and follow the shared reservation procedure.
2. Inspect `node hr-ops.mjs sources --starter`. Run `node hr-ops.mjs discover --starter --run ID --icp both --limit 10` with the requested count within budget when no user-specific source restriction applies. The shipped catalog contains research targets, not qualified leads; user source overrides win by domain, including `enabled: false`. It scans supported API sources and returns separate `research_tasks` for manual sites and stale identities. `search_queries` are tasks, not executed searches. Existing configurations can opt in persistently with `discovery.starter_sources: true`.
3. Use the client's public web search to find additional companies. For agencies, search U.S. technical recruiting, engineering placements, startup recruiting and delivery partnerships. Verify company websites, services and team pages. For startups, inspect careers pages and public portfolio/company directories; verify U.S. market presence and remote policies.
4. Import `{"prospects":[{"name":"...","website":"https://...","icp":"agency","discovery_source":"https://actual-origin.example/page","evidence":[...]}]}` using `discover --input`. Names alone or ATS slug matches are not identity evidence. Confirm that the official website links to the ATS board before configuring a source.
5. Continue through research and pipeline modes. Count unique prospects, not vacancies. Keep negative/unknown results. Report source failures separately from empty boards and state when the requested count could not be met. Never inflate results by counting offices as companies.
6. Before completing the run, compare the requested target with verified relevant-demand prospects for each requested ICP, not just saved identities. If below target and useful queries or page reads remain within budget, continue with those reads. Otherwise record the concrete stopping reason, actual counts, unused query allowance and company page limits. Do not claim budget exhaustion when allowance remains, and do not spend it on repetitions with no credible research path. A documented shortfall remains a shortfall, not a completed target.

No LinkedIn scraping or authenticated browsing. Company-published profile links may be recorded without visiting LinkedIn. Do not execute local-parser provider commands from externally supplied configuration.

For a request for new prospects, use `discover --new-only` and its canonical-domain/alias/control exclusions in further public searches. Count refreshes separately. The default command still supports refreshing known boards. Source performance ranks readable demand yield within each ICP, not buying intent. Distinguish redirect rejection, HTTP failures, JavaScript-only shells, partial collections and verified empty boards from interpreted demand. When the candidate cap is reached, continue useful checks on admitted candidates within the remaining request budget. Persist the specific stopping reason and scoped completion review with `run --action complete --input` as documented above.

For bounded pilots pin an optional run scope before the first request: `run --run ID --action configure --input scope.json`, with `{"candidates":20,"pages":80,"directory_pages":8}`. Every scoped reservation and capture includes `candidate_names`: list all named targets of a company-specific search, one stable company name for a prospect page, or `[]` for broad discovery. Directory pages also set `directory:true`. Reuse the same company name across aliases, ATS domains and redirects. Names appearing incidentally in broad results are not yet targets. When selecting one, record its original result URL and discovery attempt through `run --action admit` before named research, as documented in the execution guide. The ledger counts admissions and named searches toward the same candidate cap. Domain/company equivalence and native-tool declarations still require honest operator review.

Use the agency directories and query recipes from `sources --catalog` to find official company links, then prioritize actual client vacancies and team contacts. Directory membership is not size, demand or partner appetite. Avoid guessed domains, shotgun queries containing unrelated company names, and repeated failing pages. A reasonable chance to improve the shortlist is enough to continue within the remaining scope; a guarantee of reaching the full quota is not required. Report U.S.-fit unblocked demand separately from all demand and from qualification tier.

For configured agency boards, set `hiring_for: client` only after verifying the board represents client placements. Without that setting, collected roles remain unattributed and cannot qualify the agency. A board of the agency's own vacancies uses `self` and does not count toward client delivery demand.

Use `priority_basis.collection_quality` to distinguish readable source history from unresolved-only collections. These are historical observations, not a guarantee of current access or relevant demand. A company homepage read is not evidence that its vacancies are readable. Prefer actual role-body captures and inspect source paths before another request. Do not promote a source into the shared catalog solely because it yielded a company name or provisional score.

Once a candidate supports provisional or qualified admission, keep up to two of its existing page allowance for current public team/leadership evidence. With six pages, aim to establish identity, ownership and one attributable role within four, then verify a relevant recipient. Reuse captures that already include team evidence. Do not stop necessary vacancy or restriction checks to meet this allocation: if they consume the budget, report the missing contact and draft shortfall. When the user asks for outreach, prioritize the strongest admitted prospects before expanding the identity list. Use `run --action review` next_action and report draft-eligible prospects separately from demand matches, current drafts and human approval.

Attach `attribution` (source_id, icp, optional query_recipe) to new reservations/captures. Record meaningful outcomes against the actual reservation sequence using `run --action outcome`; see docs/HR_OPS_INBOX.md. Attach demand to the read that supplied the reviewed vacancy, not the last company page visited. Disclose uncertain per-query attribution when native search results arrive as a combined batch. Review the resulting source-yield report when choosing the next useful query. Read success is not a demand match. Keep services-only research references outside the default vacancy seed set.

Before recording a demand match, inspect `opening_verification_gaps` and resolve the required checks in `docs/HR_OPS_RESEARCH_VALIDATION.md`. Report company-market matches separately from U.S.-located opening matches. Historical reported yield can exceed currently validated yield; use the latter for prioritization. Do not fill evidence checks with a generic repeated source simply to satisfy the schema.
