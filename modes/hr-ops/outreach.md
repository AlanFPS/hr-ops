# Draft outreach

Read `docs/HR_OPS_RESEARCH_REVIEW.md`. Include `quality.opening_ids` for every opening used in the message. Multiple confidential agency searches do not imply one shared client: prefer "either search" or future partnerships, not "that client". Different posting IDs or titles are not proof of distinct hires. Review implicit employer relationships and quantities as carefully as explicit claims. Do not write boilerplate review attestations to get past validation.

Keep recipient-role reasoning in review metadata when spelling it out would make the message mechanical. Do not write "your website lists you as..." merely to satisfy the checklist. Verify a uniquely identifiable recipient before calling a draft ready for human use; a first name and title alone can support a research draft but require identity clarification. Never invent a surname, profile URL or email. Prefer one short signal and one exploratory question for LinkedIn.

Run `outreach COMPANY` or `outreach --top 3` for briefs, then actually write the requested drafts. If fewer qualify, say how many and why; never relax the rubric silently. Pick a current relevant contact from the brief.

Produce email and LinkedIn variants unless the user specifies one channel. Include: actual hiring signal, why this recipient, a plausible LATAM service benefit, and one useful question. Avoid generic praise, urgency, assumed pain, invented track records or unsolicited commitments. Use only confirmed firm services. Omit unspecified fees, guarantee terms and candidate availability.

Save each draft using the documented claims checklist, channel, purpose, contact ID and current revision. Every factual phrase has an observed source or a configured firm field; hypotheses are phrased as possibilities/questions. A validator cannot prove entailment: review each claim against its evidence before import.

When correcting or shortening an existing draft, include `replaces_draft_id` with the old draft's ID. This preserves its history but makes the old wording non-actionable. Replacement must match channel, purpose, recipient and follow-up interaction. Omit this field only for intentional alternatives. Never present superseded wording as the current recommendation.

Before saving, read the complete message in the sender's voice. Check who each role, pronoun and company affiliation belongs to: a prospect's CTO title describes the recipient, never the sender. For example, use "Given your role as CTO at Acme..." only with recipient evidence; "I'm reaching out as CTO at Acme..." would falsely claim that role for the sender. Substring citations alone do not validate the meaning of the complete sentence. Keep unconfigured sender credentials out of the message. Correct content errors before saving; fixture count targets never justify leaving a known false claim in a draft. If a bad draft was already saved, report it as unsafe historical content and prepare a corrected draft through the supported workflow rather than rewriting canonical JSON or pretending the bad version passed.

Missing firm name or sender name does not block writing a review draft, but it prevents ready status. Email drafts also require a configured firm email and an evidenced public recipient email to become ready; LinkedIn drafts do not require email addresses. A prospect can be `OUTREACH_READY` because its LinkedIn draft is complete while its email draft remains incomplete. Ready never means approved or sent. Do not invent placeholders as facts. Tell the user what remains missing for each channel. Show the messages and their factual basis; do not end with only the CLI brief. This drafting mode never sends. Only an explicit assisted-send request routes to `linkedin-send.md`; approval of prose alone does not execute it.

Inspect existing current drafts before generating more. Use `intent: exploratory` for PROVISIONAL and `qualified` for QUALIFIED. Exploratory drafts cannot assert verified eligibility, pain or buying intent. Supply `quality` with `signal_claim` matching an observed claim, `hypothesis_claim` matching a supported hypothesis claim, `recipient_rationale`, `next_step` present in the body, and `agent_reviewed: true` only after checking the complete message. See the JSON example in docs/HR_OPS.md. These are structural and recorded review checks, not semantic proof.

No em dashes in new prose. CUP Latam LinkedIn drafts normally use one or two short sentences within the configurable 300-character editorial ceiling. Preserve source quotations and historical text.
