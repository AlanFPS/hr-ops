# Qualify and explain

Run `qualify COMPANY`, then inspect `status COMPANY --markdown`. The score is a configurable priority heuristic, not a probability. Do not supply a score manually or modify thresholds to force a desired prospect through.

Explain demand, organization fit, LATAM compatibility, service fit/recruiting need, and contact accessibility with citations. Qualification needs current attributable demand, observed U.S. market presence, a supported service-fit hypothesis and positive compatibility evidence, as well as score/coverage thresholds. Unknown compatibility and U.S.-only restrictions stay in research. A verified out-of-market company or already-LATAM-specialist agency is disqualified with evidence.

If researching a previously contacted company changes the recommendation, explain the change without erasing its commercial state. Configuration calibration must be explicit and retain before/after reasoning.
