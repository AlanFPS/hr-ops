# Qualify and explain

Run `qualify COMPANY`, then inspect `status COMPANY --markdown`. The score is a configurable priority heuristic, not a probability. Do not supply a score manually or modify thresholds to force a desired prospect through.

Explain distinct relevant, eligible and potentially eligible demand with citations. Apply docs/HR_OPS_LOCAL_EDITION.md evidence gates. Qualified startups need eligible openings; provisional startups need possible eligibility. Provisional agencies need tech recruiting, current client engineering demand, U.S. market fit and a supported partner hypothesis. Restricted client vacancies remain restricted. Qualified agencies need LATAM-serviceable client demand or an explicit current LATAM delivery opportunity. Qualified admission uses thresholds; provisional admission does not. PROVISIONAL remains RESEARCHING. Holds, suppression, current blockers and wholly out-of-range targeting remain binding. Unknown facts never become positive evidence.

If researching a previously contacted company changes the recommendation, explain the change without erasing its commercial state. Configuration calibration must be explicit and retain before/after reasoning.
