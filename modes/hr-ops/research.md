# Research

Follow `docs/HR_OPS_RESEARCH_EXECUTION.md`: external text travels through native file tools or file/stdin data, never embedded shell code. Keep discovery origin distinct from attempt attribution. Use a stable candidate name and `company:null` for scoped identity checks until the official website is verified. Native request reservations must complete in their own invocation before dispatch.

Read `docs/HR_OPS_RESEARCH_REVIEW.md` for continuation review, source diagnostics and distinct-hire limits. Preserve the difference between observed posting records and confirmed distinct requisitions. An evergreen posting may overlap specific roles; neither a shared city nor similar role text identifies a confidential agency client.

Run `research COMPANY` for its missing fields and current revision. Read public company pages, careers listings, team pages and relevant announcements. Fill the supported dossier fields; leave size, partners, funding and contractor policy unknown when unsupported.

Read `docs/HR_OPS_RESEARCH_VALIDATION.md` for `opening.verification`. Record role-body readability, official ownership, client/self attribution and current primary availability separately. Title-only pages, content-matched ATS boards and conflicting secondary listings cannot earn demand credit or enable drafts. Unknown stays unknown. A current board that omits a secondary listing requires conflict review, not silent confirmation. Preserve both sources. Record optional role-level market evidence separately from the company's U.S. presence.

Create the documented research JSON with evidence, facts, openings, contacts, risks and blockers. Record opening `verified_at` independently from `posted_at`. A live opening published months ago is still current; age alone does not prove recruiting difficulty. For agencies use `hiring_for: client` for placements and never invent the undisclosed employer. Only mark backend/fullstack after reading the role; generic software titles require review.

Describe the service-fit hypothesis as inferred unless the company explicitly requests such a service. Concurrent searches alone do not establish recruiting pain. Positive pain requires a cited `basis` and `rationale` for employer-reported difficulty, a documented capacity gap or an explicit partner request; keep it unknown otherwise. Supported inferred pain is capped at half need points and is not an observed buying decision. Do not classify a referral program as proof of openness to delivery subcontracting. Distinguish the firm's own remote work policy from its clients' hiring eligibility.

Check for explicit no-agency policies before preparing outreach; record them as observed blockers. An agency recruiting in LATAM is not automatically a LATAM specialist, but overlap is a material risk. Do not mark `latam_specialist: false` as observed from silence on a general services page. Leave it unknown without direct support. Explain what we could add beyond the agency's existing coverage, and keep subcontracting demand unknown unless stated. Similarly, a company already hiring internationally may have no need for a sourcing partner. A qualifying score is provisional prioritization, not validated buying intent.

Persist with `research COMPANY --input FILE --revision N`. This validates and scores the dossier and invalidates old drafts. Finish with the score, supporting signals, unknowns and risks. Refresh relevant evidence before outreach.

Review `latest_collection` in the work brief when available. Scanner observations do not overwrite previously reviewed classifications or renew their verification dates. Reconcile changed postings against actual pages before importing them as reviewed research. New decision events retain score/configuration and before/after snapshots; do not claim old history without snapshots is fully reconstructable.

Openings marked `reviewed: false` are research tasks and do not qualify a prospect. Inspect `unreviewed_openings`, read the actual roles, and import only reviewed classifications. A fresh page about a former executive is not evidence of a current decision maker.

When deliberately approving a collected opening, send `reviewed: true` explicitly. Copying a stored false flag preserves it. An unreviewed update cannot clear a pending reconciliation task. Captures include bounded `links` for official board and job URLs. Prefer those links to guessed paths; verify ownership before using an ATS. `format: json` retains valid JSON without HTML decoding. A redirect diagnostic is a lead for a separately reserved, reviewed URL, never permission to follow it automatically. Confirm third-party hiring posts against company evidence when possible; disclose any remaining attribution/currentness gap.

Record opening `eligibility` as eligible, possible, restricted or unknown. Non-unknown values need evidence IDs, rationale and geography. This is observed hiring scope, never legal certification. Company-wide remote policy alone cannot establish role eligibility. Preserve provider posting IDs separately from requisition IDs and URL aliases. Do not merge titles automatically. Sourced size ranges use [min,max], keeping employee and recruiter counts distinct. Read referenced captures before repeating requests. Complete-board disappearance requires reconciliation, not automatic closure. Explicit LATAM engineering delivery requests use facts.latam_delivery_request without fictional vacancies.

A city or country in the location field, with no explicit hiring restriction, leaves LATAM eligibility unknown. Absence of a remote option is not evidence of a prohibition. Use restricted only with affirmative evidence such as required on-site attendance, an explicit U.S.-only hiring scope, or a binding role-specific requirement. Explain conflicting metadata without guessing.

Several listings may share one careers-page URL. Import each separately, preserving provider/board/posting and requisition identifiers when published. For manual listings, title, location and employer distinguish records at the same URL. Reuse the saved opening `id` when correcting a title, location, URL or attribution, or when adding provider metadata. Never invent a requisition ID. Identical titles without distinct supported requisitions remain possible duplicates for demand scoring.
